# Repository instructions

After every change to the SCULP Studio website, append a concise entry to `WORK_LOG.md` in the same GitHub change. Use the Toronto local date. Record what changed, the reason when useful, and any meaningful verification or limitation. Keep older entries intact and put the newest date first.

## Website design references

For future website design work, consult https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill and https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable as requested by the user. Preserve SCULP’s established visual identity and the task’s explicit scope; use their typography, responsive, motion, accessibility and rendered-review guidance as applicable.
