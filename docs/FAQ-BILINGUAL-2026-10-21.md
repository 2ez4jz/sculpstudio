## Booking & Availability / 预订与档期

### How do I book my bridal makeup and hairstyling?

Send us your wedding date, getting-ready location, preferred artist and requested services. Our team will review availability and guide you through the next steps.

**如何预订新娘化妆和发型服务？**

请提供婚礼日期、准备地点、希望预约的化妆师和所需服务。团队将确认档期并协助完成预订。

Anchor: `/faq/#book-bridal`

### How far in advance should I book?

We recommend reaching out as soon as your wedding date is confirmed, especially during peak wedding season.

**应该提前多久预订？**

建议在婚礼日期确定后尽早联系，尤其是婚礼旺季。

Anchor: `/faq/#when-to-book`

### Can I request a specific artist?

You are welcome to request your preferred SCULP artist. Availability depends on your wedding date and selected services.

**可以指定化妆师吗？**

可以指定希望预约的 SCULP 化妆师，具体取决于婚礼日期和档期。

Anchor: `/faq/#choose-artist`

### How is my booking confirmed?

Your booking is confirmed according to the terms in your booking agreement. An enquiry alone does not reserve your date.

**如何正式确认预订？**

预订以双方确认的协议及相关条件为准。仅提交询盘并不代表档期已锁定。

Anchor: `/faq/#booking-confirmation`

## Bridal Trials & Preparation / 试妆与准备

### What happens during a bridal trial?

Your trial is an opportunity to explore your preferred makeup and hairstyle, discuss inspiration and refine the details with your artist.

**试妆会进行哪些内容？**

试妆用于探索理想妆发、交流参考风格，并与化妆师调整细节。

Anchor: `/faq/#trial-experience`

### When should I schedule my bridal trial?

We recommend arranging your trial sufficiently ahead of your wedding to allow time for adjustments and planning.

**什么时候安排试妆？**

建议在婚礼前留出充分时间，以便沟通、试妆及调整。

Anchor: `/faq/#trial-timing`

### What should I bring to my trial?

Bring inspiration photos, images of your wedding dress and accessories, and any preferences or concerns you would like to discuss.

**试妆需要准备什么？**

建议准备妆发参考图、婚纱及配饰照片，以及希望沟通的个人偏好。

Anchor: `/faq/#bring-to-trial`

### Can I request a particular makeup style?

Absolutely. We encourage you to share inspiration images so your artist can tailor the look to your features and personal style.

**可以指定妆容风格吗？**

当然可以。欢迎提供参考图片，化妆师会结合个人特点和偏好设计造型。

Anchor: `/faq/#makeup-style`

## The Bridal Journey / 新娘体验旅程

### The Enquiry / 初次咨询

It begins with your day.

Share your wedding date, getting-ready location, preferred artist and services. We'll review availability and help you explore the next steps.

从您的婚礼计划开始。

提供婚礼日期、准备地点、偏好的化妆师及所需服务。团队会确认档期，并协助您了解下一步。

- Your wedding date and location / 婚礼日期与地点
- Your preferred artist and services / 偏好的化妆师与服务
- Your inspiration and initial questions / 参考风格与初步问题


### The Trial / 试妆体验

A look that feels like you.

Explore your makeup and hairstyle with your artist. Bring the references, dress details and accessories that help tell your story.

找到属于您的妆发。

与化妆师一起探索妆容和发型。准备参考图片、婚纱细节及配饰信息，让理想造型更清晰。

- Makeup and hair inspiration / 妆容与发型参考
- Your dress and accessory photos / 婚纱和配饰照片
- Personal preferences and concerns / 个人偏好与关注事项


### The Preparation / 婚前准备

Bring the details together.

Share your ceremony time, getting-ready location and service list so the beauty schedule can be planned around your celebration.

让每一处细节就位。

提供仪式时间、准备地点及服务名单，以便围绕婚礼流程安排妆发时间。

- Your ceremony and ready-by times / 仪式时间与准备完成时间
- Everyone receiving services / 需要服务的人员
- Planned looks and schedule updates / 计划造型与流程更新


### The Wedding Day / 婚礼当天

Your moment, beautifully yours.

Your artist works with your confirmed service and schedule. Discuss touch-ups and look changes in advance so the arrangements suit your day.

从容迎接属于您的时刻。

化妆师将根据已确认的服务和时间表提供服务。请提前沟通补妆及换造型需求，让安排适合您的婚礼。

- Your confirmed beauty schedule / 已确认的妆发时间表
- Your selected service and coverage / 所选服务及覆盖范围
- Any agreed touch-ups or look changes / 已约定的补妆或造型更换
