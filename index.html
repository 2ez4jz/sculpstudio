About SCULP

Beauty,
with intention.

SCULP Studio is a Toronto-based beauty studio creating refined makeup for weddings, events, commercial work, portraits, and education.

Our approach is simple: begin with the person, understand the context, and build a look that feels considered rather than imposed.

待补：About 主视觉 1 张。建议 Founder 工作状态、后台准备、妆面细节或 studio 氛围图；不建议放普通团队合照。
Our Philosophy
待老板确认：是否把 “Beauty should still feel like you.” 作为 About 页核心品牌句。

Beauty should still feel like you.

We believe makeup works best when it responds to the individual — their features, personal style, setting, clothing, light, and the way the final look will be seen both in person and on camera.

Rather than applying the same formula to every face, we focus on balance, proportion, texture, and detail.

The result should feel polished, intentional, and recognizably yours.

Our Approach

Considered from every angle.

Bridal

We consider the dress, hair, venue, photography, timing, and atmosphere of the day — not only the makeup itself.

Commercial & Portrait

We consider concept, lighting, camera, styling, and the final image so the makeup supports the whole visual direction.

Personal Beauty

We focus on what feels natural to wear, easy to maintain, and appropriate to the moment.

Different settings ask for different decisions. That is where the work begins.
